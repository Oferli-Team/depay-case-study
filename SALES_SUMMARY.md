# DePay — Short Sales Version

DePay is the public pilot component of a startup platform for cryptocurrency payments through specialized terminals. It brings merchant invoicing, wallet-linked transactions, validation, confirmation, webhook delivery, KYC/risk workflows, and operational reporting into one system.

The implementation demonstrates a service-based Go backend, a PostgreSQL data layer with enforced payment-state and audit rules, a React console for customer, merchant, compliance, and administrator roles, and configurable integrations for blockchain RPC, balances, gas, KYC, and webhooks. Docker, Kubernetes configuration, CI checks, metrics, and dashboards support local operation and deployment-oriented validation.

For a potential client, the project demonstrates Backend & DevOps experience in payment workflows where state consistency, external integrations, compliance visibility, and operational traceability matter. The project remained at pilot stage, where real transactions were tested. It is presented as previous startup work by an Oferli technical team member; the startup identity and commercial details remain confidential.
